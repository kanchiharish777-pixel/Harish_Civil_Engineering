M25 Concrete Enhancement

Research Project • Sustainable Concrete Technology

Project Overview

Developed sustainable M25 concrete using silica fume and recycled coarse aggregate, achieving 12% higher compressive strength through extensive laboratory testing and optimized mix design.

Research Scope

  • Developed sustainable M25 concrete mixes.
  • Used silica fume as supplementary cementitious material.
  • Incorporated recycled coarse aggregate.
  • Performed 90+ laboratory tests and compressive strength evaluation.
  • Compared conventional and modified concrete performance.

Research Responsibilities

Prepared concrete mix designs and trial batches.
Conducted cube casting, curing and strength testing.
Recorded and analyzed laboratory observations.
Documented methodology and final research results.

Outcome

The optimized sustainable M25 concrete achieved approximately 12% higher compressive strength while promoting environmentally responsible construction using recycled materials.
